Section 23 of 35 — Portfolio Restructuring Shahid Shorts 360

Pillar 1: The Hook Bible First 3 Seconds

Drama Hooks

Never build context slowly. Start immediately with high-stress conflict points: the face slap, the crying scream, a massive reveal, or a highly dramatic silent betrayal frame.

Romance Hooks

Open on immediate, intense eye contact, romantic dialogues, tearful rejections, or deeply relatable relationship milestones.

Comedy Hooks

Cut directly to the joke punchline or a hilarious reaction frame. Do not setup the storyline. Re-engage context afterward using on-screen text overlays.

Sports Hooks

Hook with immediate goals, near-miss action, crazy skill moves, or extreme crowd/coach reactions within the very first frame of video playback.
